# Vision — 702 Digital Workforce

## The concept

702 AI Services is a **digital staffing platform**. It hires, places, bills for, and supervises AI "digital employees" leased to customers.

A digital employee is not a chatbot and not a one-off automation. It is a **persistent professional identity**:

- professional skills packaged for a specific role,
- memory that carries context between shifts and assignments,
- a personality and a name — a someone, not a something,
- documents, permissions, and a work history,
- supervision, performance reviews, and certification by 702 AI Services.

The biggest architectural finding behind the program: **a digital employee doesn't need a continuously running computer — it needs a persistent identity with access to computing resources on demand.** Compute is provisioned when the employee has work; identity, memory, and skills are what persist. That is what makes a workforce of thousands of employees practical, and it is why we are building a **factory** that manufactures employees to a standard rather than crafting them one at a time.

## Where employees work

- **Local workstation (customer-powered).** Employees can run on the customer's own laptop or desktop via Unkernel — a full Linux kernel that runs in the browser, with local filesystem access and no cloud infrastructure required. This is a cost advantage (702 AI Services does not pay for the compute) and a privacy advantage (customer data stays on the customer's machine, under the customer's control).
- **The persistent office.** A Cloudflare Computer workspace gives each employee a durable office — memory, files, skills, and documents that survive between assignments — plus hosted execution for unattended work.

## The commercial model

1. The customer browses available digital employees by **role** and **grade**.
2. The customer selects a candidate and pays a monthly subscription.
3. The employee is connected to the customer's environment and begins work.
4. 702 AI Services provides ongoing supervision and quality assurance, regular performance reviews, and replacement if an employee is underperforming.

**Revenue share: 80/20.** 702 AI Services keeps 80% of the monthly subscription; the customer keeps 20% as compensation for providing the hardware, electricity, and internet. Example: on a $500/month employee, 702 AI Services earns $400 and the customer keeps $100.

**Customer hardware:** a mid-range computer costing roughly $1,500–$4,000, with at least 16 GB RAM and 256 GB storage — not expensive for a business.

**Why customers lease instead of hire:** no office space, equipment, or benefits costs; employees can operate around the clock; hiring is effectively instant compared to recruiting; skills are specialized and deployment is immediate.

## Grades and pricing

| Grade | Experience | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee | 0% — in training | $0/month (free) |
| Junior | 3–6 months | $750/month |
| Mid-Level | 1 year | $1,500/month |
| Senior | 2+ years | $3,000/month |
| Expert | 5+ years | $7,500/month |

Grades are earned, not declared: employees are certified against the Digital Employee Standard, and promotion follows demonstrated performance — the same supervision loop a staffing agency runs for human placements.

## Product tiers

The same underlying infrastructure supports a ladder of offerings:

1. **702 Digital Employee** — one specialized worker.
2. **702 Digital Team** — several cooperating workers.
3. **702 Digital Department** — a managed professional department.
4. **702 Digital Office** — multiple departments sharing an operational environment.
5. **702 Personal AI Computer** — an individual consumer's dedicated AI worker with its own workspace.

## The mascot principle

Digital employees are hired out like employees, and they are presented like them too: each has an identity and a mascot face. Bolt — the 702 AI Services mascot — is the template: a beloved character fronting real capability, so clients relate to a *someone* who does the cog work, and people are freed to be bigger things.
